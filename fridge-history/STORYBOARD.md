## Through-line

One continuous action, "bring the shopping home and keep it until dinner", carries the viewer through time. **Two shape-based transitions** bracket the minute:
- **Into the past (0:07):** the fridge door wipes across the frame and the past kitchen is already behind it.
- **Back to the present (0:54):** the cellar shelf rows line up with the fridge shelves.

The hero, his mustard sweater and the three products (milk, meat, butter) stay the same in both eras, so the viewer always knows where to look.
