## Description
Could 25 countries match the population of China? We build a team one country at a time — from Ireland to the Philippines — and watch a shared progress bar climb toward China’s 2025 population estimate of about 1.416 billion.

This is a selected lineup, not the minimum number of countries needed. After Nigeria, 24 countries reach 92.5% of China’s figure. The Philippines takes the team to 100.8% — about 10.9 million people more than China in this dataset.

Population figures are mid-2025 estimates (UN World Population Prospects 2024). Calculations use unrounded values; numbers on screen are rounded for display. Map boundaries are shown neutrally and do not imply any position on disputed territories.
