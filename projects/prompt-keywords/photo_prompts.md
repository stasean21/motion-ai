# Промпты для 14 фото (ChatGPT / GPT Image)

## Как генерировать, чтобы кроссовок был один и тот же
1. Сначала сгенерируй **00 CANON** и сохрани его.
2. Каждое следующее фото: **прикрепи CANON как референс** и начни промпт с фразы:
   `Use the attached sneaker as the exact reference: same shoe, same design, same colors, same proportions.`
3. Формат всех фото: **вертикальный 4:5** (1024×1280 или крупнее). Кроссовок в профиль, носок вправо, по центру, занимает около 70% ширины.
   Исключения по позиции: 06 macro и 07 lowangle.
4. Без логотипов, брендов, текста и водяных знаков. Сохраняй файлы под номерами ниже.

**Описание кроссовка (вставляется в каждый промпт):**
`a minimalist white knit-mesh running sneaker, side profile facing right, white foam midsole, light grey rubber outsole, white flat laces, one bright orange (#FF6A00) heel pull-tab, no logos, no text`

---

**00 CANON / boring** — исходник и «скучное фото» хука
`Photo of [SNEAKER] on a plain light grey background, flat on-camera flash, harsh small shadow directly under the shoe, slightly dull colors, amateur product snapshot, centered, vertical 4:5.`

**00b slightly better** — «Make it look better» (почти без изменений)
`Same photo as the reference, only slightly brighter and a bit more contrast; same background, same flash shadow, same position. Vertical 4:5.`

**01a messy table** — «до» для productshot
`[SNEAKER] standing on a wooden kitchen table with distracting clutter around it: a mug, papers, a phone, a window behind; casual indoor light, everyday snapshot. Same shoe position, vertical 4:5.`

**01 /productshot**
`Clean commercial product photography of [SNEAKER] on a seamless light grey studio background, soft natural contact shadow under the sole, even studio lighting, sharp focus, centered. Vertical 4:5.`

**02 /softlight**
`[SNEAKER] on a seamless light background, large diffused soft light, gentle soft shadows, smooth tonal transitions across the white mesh, airy and calm. Same position. Vertical 4:5.`

**03 /hardlight**
`[SNEAKER] on a seamless white background, single small hard light from the upper left, crisp sharp diagonal shadow falling to the lower right, high contrast, bold graphic look. Same position. Vertical 4:5.`

**04 /rimlight**
`[SNEAKER] on a dark charcoal grey background, strong back light creating a thin bright illuminated edge along the heel, the top line and the toe, subject separated from the background, realistic reflected light on the material (no neon outline). Same position. Vertical 4:5.`

**05 /goldenhour**
`[SNEAKER] standing on a light stone surface outdoors at golden hour, warm low evening sunlight from the side, long soft shadow, warm sky tones in the softly blurred background, the shoe stays recognizably white. Same position. Vertical 4:5.`

**06 /macro**
`Extreme macro close-up of the same sneaker: knit mesh weave texture, a stitched seam and an edge of the orange heel tab in frame, shallow depth of field with focus on the seam, crisp texture. Vertical 4:5.`

**07 /lowangle**
`[SNEAKER] photographed from a low angle at ground level, camera slightly below the sole, the shoe looks bold and imposing, whole shoe in frame with space around toe and heel, mild perspective (no extreme wide-angle distortion), clean light studio floor. Vertical 4:5.`

**08a clutter** — «до» для minimal
`[SNEAKER] on a light studio set surrounded by distracting props: a shoe box, folded fabric, geometric decor shapes (sphere, cone, cube), busy composition. Same position. Vertical 4:5.`

**08 /minimal**
`Minimal composition: [SNEAKER] alone on a plain off-white background with lots of negative space, soft shadow, nothing else in frame. Same position. Vertical 4:5.`

**09 /editorial**
`Editorial magazine-style still life: [SNEAKER] placed on a simple beige sculptural pedestal, one bold terracotta arch-shaped prop on the right, asymmetric but balanced composition, warm neutral backdrop, directional light that shapes the form. No text, no logos, no magazine branding. Vertical 4:5.`

**10 /motionblur**
`[SNEAKER] perfectly sharp in the center while the background shows strong horizontal motion blur streaks moving left to right, sense of speed. Keep the shoe sharp. Vertical 4:5.`
