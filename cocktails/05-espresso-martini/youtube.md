**Title**
Espresso Martini in 30 seconds ☕ The 5 : 3 : 2 recipe

**Description**
Coffee? Cocktail? Both. The midnight classic — vodka, fresh espresso and coffee liqueur, shaken hard for that velvet crema.

Recipe:
• vodka — 50 ml (1⅔ oz)
• fresh espresso, cooled — 30 ml (1 oz)
• coffee liqueur — 20 ml (⅔ oz)
• shake hard with ice
• double strain into a chilled martini glass
• 3 coffee beans on top — health, wealth & happiness

Save it for the weekend.

18+ Please drink responsibly.
