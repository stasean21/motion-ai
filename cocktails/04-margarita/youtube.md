**Title**
Margarita in 30 seconds 🍋‍🟩 The 2 : 1 : 1 recipe

**Description**
Salt. Lime. Tequila. The Mexican classic — just remember 2 : 1 : 1.

Recipe:
• salt the rim of a coupe
• tequila — 50 ml (1⅔ oz)
• orange liqueur — 25 ml (⅚ oz)
• fresh lime juice — 25 ml (⅚ oz)
• shake hard with ice, 15 seconds
• strain into the glass
• lime wheel

Save it for the weekend.

18+ Please drink responsibly.
