# 04 · Margarita — EN only · kinetic type

34 s · 120 BPM (beat = 0.5 s, bar = 2 s) · 1080×1920 · 60 fps · AAC

## Idea

**The words become the drink.** Every ingredient is first a big word on screen. Once it has been read, the word drops into the mixing glass and turns into a layer of liquid. SHAKE rattles like ice, STRAIN pours letter by letter, WHEEL spins around the lime. Text is the hero; glass, shaker and liquid answer the words.

Series DNA stays: wine velvet, gold Art Deco frame, smooth spline camera.
Type contrast:
- **Limelight**: heavy Art Deco display, used for nouns and numbers.
- **Poiret One**: hairline, used for units and connectors.
- **Great Vibes**: script, used for the name and the soft lines.

Each phrase has one key word in **lime `#C8F05A`**. Everything else is cream `#F1E4CF` or soft `#C9AE8E`.

Stage:
- The coupe is at world x 540.
- The mixing glass is at world x 1300.
- The camera trucks between them, and the text layer rides on top with its own parallax.
- Foreground: soft lime bokeh drifting faster than the velvet, giving three depth planes.

Reading rule: every word is fully still for ≥ 0.7 s; a phrase is never replaced faster than it can be read aloud.

## Recipe (2 : 1 : 1)

Salt the rim · Tequila 50 ml · Orange liqueur 25 ml · Lime juice 25 ml · Ice, shake hard 15 s · Strain into the coupe · Lime wheel

## Sound

The groove is a light Latin bossa: chords and bass from the series, plus a son clave woodblock (3-2) and a maraca shaker on 16ths from bar 3.
- **Word slams:** soft low thumps on the beat. Not every word gets one; only the stack and the amounts.
- **Salt:** a grain hiss.
- **Word drops:** liquid plops.
- **Shake:** an ice rattle with the 16ths.
- **Strain:** a pour.
- **Wheel:** a whoosh, then a tick.
- **Chimes:** at 24 s, 28 s and 32 s.

## Risk check before the full render

The main risk is the readability of the drop moments: a word shrinking into the glass must already be read. Check: a beat contact sheet plus a dense frame sequence around 12–14 s.
