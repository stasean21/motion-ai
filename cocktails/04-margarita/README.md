# 04 · Margarita (EN only)

34 s · 120 BPM · 1080×1920 · 60 fps · kinetic type. The direction, scene variants and choices are in [STORYBOARD.md](STORYBOARD.md).

Recipe, 2 : 1 : 1:
1. Salt the rim.
2. Tequila 50 ml.
3. Orange liqueur 25 ml.
4. Lime juice 25 ml.
5. Shake hard with ice for 15 s.
6. Strain into a coupe.
7. Garnish with a lime wheel.

The text is the hero: every ingredient word drops into the mixing glass and becomes a layer. SALT crumbles into the salt rim, SHAKE rattles, STRAIN pours letter by letter, and LIME WHEEL orbits the lime before snapping into a line.

Fonts:
- Limelight: heavy Art Deco.
- Poiret One: hairline.
- Great Vibes: script.

Key word colour: lime `#C8F05A`.

Files: `out/margarita.mp4`, `out/storyboard-en.jpg`, `out/keyframes.jpg`.

Build: `node beats.mjs 0.35 en` (storyboard), `node render.mjs margarita en`.
