# Reference breakdown → flat cartoon mockup for the cocktail series

Source: `example.mp4` (user upload). 22.9 s, 720×720, 60 fps, with audio. The picture is 16:9 (720×405) inside a square with black bars top and bottom.

Method: 4 fps contact sheets of the whole clip, full-resolution key frames, dense 10 fps sequences around every transition, and an audio onset map. I did not listen to the audio, so the music character is not assessed.

## Style: what to carry over

- **Drawing.** Flat vector with no outlines. Form comes from one darker tone shape (a shadow side on faces and bodies) plus one light highlight shape. Corners are rounded and geometry simplified.
- **Palette.** Saturated and cool-dominant (lavender, violet, cyan, mint, lime), with hot accents (magenta, red, orange). Backgrounds are low-contrast pastels, so characters and props pop.
- **Characters.**
  - Big heads and chunky jaw shapes.
  - Glasses as a strong graphic accent.
  - Eyes are white ellipses with small dark pupils and flat lids.
  - Hands are simple mittens.
  - Mascots are bean-shaped birds with huge white eyes, small triangle beaks, leaf crests and **orange rubber-hose legs**.
- **Light as a graphic.** Translucent cones, radial burst rays, glow blobs, 4-point sparkle stars. Light changes the colour of whatever it hits (the birds' eyes go yellow).
- **Storytelling.** Each scene has one clear object of attention (dish → virus → tube → lab). Scale is used for drama (a tiny figure against a giant object), and secondary characters react to the hero object.
- **Motion.**
  - Iris wipe out of a round object (match cut).
  - Hard cuts on beats.
  - A panel slide into a split screen.
  - Slow crane reveals.
  - Overshoot on entrances.
  - Idle bobbing so nothing is ever fully still.

## Transfer to the cocktail series (mockup, Tequila Sunrise)

| Reference | Cocktail version |
|---|---|
| Scientist with a petri dish | **Bartender** (bob haircut, magenta glasses, violet vest, bow tie) behind the bar; a highball on the counter fires the light cone |
| Two bird mascots | **Toucan** (violet, huge orange beak) and **flamingo-bird** (red/magenta, mint crest). They frame the bar in the foreground with eyes glowing in the drink's light, then fly in happy on the hero shot |
| Iris out of the dish | Iris out of the **glass rim** into the world inside the drink |
| Tiny human vs giant virus | Tiny bartender on the glossy floor vs a **giant grenadine sun** rising on the horizon; ice cubes float like icebergs and the orange slice hangs like a planet. "GRENADINE sinks like the sun" |
| Glove + test tube + rays | Teal glove raising the **finished Tequila Sunrise** against rays and sparkles, birds celebrating |
| Panel slide to split screen | A diagonal seam split: **90 ml ORANGE JUICE** pour on top, the bartender adding **+ GRENADINE** below |

Type: the reference has none. For the series I added **Fredoka Bold** (rounded, OFL) with an offset coloured shadow, which matches the rounded flat shapes. One key word per phrase is in a hot colour.

Format: 9:16 (series standard), not the reference's letterboxed 16:9.
