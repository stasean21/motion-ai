## EN — файл out/mojito-en.mp4

**Title**
Mojito in 30 seconds 🌿 The 50 · 20 · 100 formula

**Description**
The Cuban classic from Havana — mint, lime, rum and lots of crushed ice.

Recipe:
• mint — 10 leaves, ½ lime in wedges
• sugar syrup — 20 ml (⅔ oz)
• muddle gently
• crushed ice — to the top
• white rum — 50 ml (1⅔ oz)
• soda water — 100 ml (3⅓ oz)
• mint sprig and a lime wheel

Skip the rum and top up with soda for a Virgin Mojito.

Save it for the weekend.

18+ Please drink responsibly.
