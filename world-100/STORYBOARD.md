# «Если бы на Земле жили 100 человек» · first minute · storyboard

Format: 16:9 (1920×1080), the first 1:00. Style: the flat no-sharp-corners language (shared `lib.js`), with **one identical little person = 1%**. Each person's body colour = continent, assigned once the people are distributed.

Files:
- `storyboard.jpg`: the board with captions.
- `panels/1–8.png`: key frames.
- `board.html`, `panels.js`, `lib.js`: editable SVG sources.

## Through-line

**The same 100 people never leave the screen.** They go dots → grid → map → plaza → one person → the hundred again. Each statistic is a *movement* of people, not a chart. Colour is assigned on the map and kept afterwards, so in shots 6–8 you still see where everyone came from.

## Shots: variants → choice

| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | a) a dot cloud around the globe with a fast pull-out · b) a city-lights night Earth · c) a counter racing to 8 000 000 000 | **a**: speed streaks and a dense cloud say "too many to imagine" in 2 s |
| 2 | 0:08–0:16 | a) the cloud condenses into a 10×10 grid · b) people drop in one by one · c) a stadium | **a**: the clean grid reads "100" instantly; title + "1 = 1%" chip; a tick per person in the sound |

| 4 | 0:26–0:32 | a) people walk dotted arcs to their continent · b) people teleport · c) a sand-pour fill | **a**: walking makes the ratio *felt*: the Asia stream is visibly the longest |
| 5 | 0:32–0:39 | a) counters + a 100-segment bar · b) a pie chart · c) continents scale to population | **a + bar**: counters over each group, then the bottom bar sums to 100% |
| 6 | 0:39–0:49 | a) the map folds back into a plaza, city vs village, people cross over · b) a split screen · c) a zoom into a city | **a**: buildings grow on one side and houses on the other; the crowd divides ≈57 / ≈43 |
| 7 | 0:49–0:55 | a) a push-in on one person with orbiting icons (age, language, internet) · b) an ID card · c) a speech bubble | **a**: the personal close-up turns statistics into a person. Three soft "pops" |
| 8 | 0:55–1:00 | a) pull back to the grid: kids are shorter, elders have grey hair, a Wi‑Fi mark for online + three chips · b) a plain title | **a**: it previews the episode's next chapters inside the same hundred |
