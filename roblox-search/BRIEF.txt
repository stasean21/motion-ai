Animation and screen design

Use a flat 2D world map, a readable flag character, a location name, a numeric index and a bar. Keep the footer visible: GOOGLE SEARCH INTEREST · JAN–SEP 2026. Keep a smaller label visible: INDEX 0–100 · NOT PLAYER COUNTS. Use a consistent map projection and current neutral map assets; disputed boundaries must follow the same documented mapping policy throughout.

All bars use the same 0–100 scale. Keep area on the map separate from the index: land size must not change the bar value. Use a small inset for compact locations such as Hong Kong or Singapore. Place labels outside crowded regions and connect them with a leader line.

Five second location cycle

0.0–0.6 seconds: pan to the location and pulse its outline. 0.6–1.2: reveal the flag and name. 1.2–2.0: animate the index bar to its exact exported value. 2.0–4.5: hold the completed number and name. 4.5–5.0: transition into the next location. Keep the completed value visible in the ranking rail through the transition.

For equal indexes, show TIED and the shared rank. Keep a common rank visible while cycling tied locations. Do not use winner reactions or rising scores to imply differences inside a tie. For untied values, a short surprised reaction is enough; avoid flag characters celebrating a larger population or more players.

Eight second final cycle

Use one second to locate, one second to reveal, four seconds to hold and compare, then two seconds for the podium update and transition. The final location receives a full map pullback and the label HIGHEST RELATIVE SEARCH INTEREST. Avoid MOST PLAYERS or BIGGEST ROBLOX COUNTRY.
