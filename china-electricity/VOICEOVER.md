# Episode 03 — Voiceover script (EN)

Тайминги совпадают с роликом `anim/out/china-electricity.mp4`. Текст взят из брифа без изменений. Шаги стран длятся по 10 с, у Франции, Канады, Бразилии, Японии и США — по 20 с.

| Time | Shot | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | The challenge | How many countries would it take to match China’s electricity generation? We’ll combine twenty-five selected countries, one by one, and see when their annual output crosses China’s total. |
| 0:15–0:30 | Unit and rules | The target is about ten thousand twenty-five terawatt-hours in twenty twenty-four. One terawatt-hour equals a billion kilowatt-hours. We’re comparing electricity generated over the year, not consumption or power-plant capacity. Let’s build the team. |
| 0:30 | Ireland | Ireland starts us off with thirty-one point one terawatt-hours. That is just a tiny sliver of our target. Next comes Denmark. |
| 0:40 | Denmark | Denmark adds thirty-five point two terawatt-hours. Two countries are on the map, and we are still below one percent of China’s total. |
| 0:50 | New Zealand | New Zealand contributes forty-four terawatt-hours. Our combined output passes one hundred, but China’s target is more than ten thousand. Keep watching the scale. |
| 1:00 | Portugal | Portugal adds forty-seven point three terawatt-hours. Four countries together bring us to about one hundred fifty-eight. There is still a long way to go. |
| 1:10 | Belgium | Belgium contributes seventy-six terawatt-hours. Five countries now give us about two hundred thirty-four. That is only a little over two percent of the target. |
| 1:20 | Switzerland | Switzerland adds seventy-eight point four terawatt-hours. The counter moves past three hundred. The country count grows faster than the progress bar. |
| 1:30 | Finland | Finland brings eighty-two point five terawatt-hours. Seven countries together are still below four hundred, less than four percent of China’s annual generation. |
| 1:40 | Netherlands | The Netherlands contributes one hundred twenty-two point three terawatt-hours. Our combined output finally passes five hundred. We are just over five percent of the way. |
| 1:50 | Norway | Norway adds one hundred fifty-seven point one terawatt-hours. The total keeps climbing, but we are still below seven percent of our target. |
| 2:00 | Poland | Poland contributes one hundred seventy-two point one terawatt-hours. Ten countries now produce about eight hundred forty-six. We haven’t reached ten percent yet. |
| 2:10 | Sweden | Sweden adds almost exactly the same amount as Poland. This time we pass one thousand terawatt-hours, and finally cross the ten-percent mark. |
| 2:20 | Thailand | Thailand contributes two hundred seventeen point three terawatt-hours. The individual additions are getting larger, but we still have most of the bar left to fill. |
| 2:30 | South Africa | South Africa adds two hundred forty-seven point five terawatt-hours. Thirteen countries now give us almost fifteen percent of China’s annual electricity output. |
| 2:40 | Italy | Italy contributes two hundred sixty-seven point six terawatt-hours. The team moves past seventeen hundred, while the quarter mark is still well ahead. |
| 2:50 | Spain | Spain adds two hundred eighty point nine terawatt-hours. We cross two thousand. Fifteen countries together have reached about one-fifth of the target. |
| 3:00 | Australia | Australia contributes two hundred eighty-three point nine terawatt-hours. Its huge area fills the map, but our progress bar measures electricity, not land. |
| 3:10 | United Kingdom | The United Kingdom adds two hundred eighty-four point five terawatt-hours. We finally cross a quarter of China’s total, after combining seventeen countries. |
| 3:20 | Vietnam | Vietnam contributes three hundred ten point six terawatt-hours. The team approaches three thousand, but we are still below thirty percent of our target. |
| 3:30 | Türkiye | Turkey adds three hundred forty-one point two terawatt-hours. We pass three thousand. Nineteen countries now produce a little under a third of China’s total. |
| 3:40 | Germany | Germany contributes four hundred ninety-one point eight terawatt-hours. Twenty countries together reach about thirty-seven percent. Even now, the halfway mark is ahead of us. |
| 3:50–4:10 | France | France adds five hundred sixty-four point five terawatt-hours. Our team reaches about four thousand three hundred. That is roughly forty-three percent of China’s annual output. We have passed four thousand, but we still haven’t reached halfway. |
| 4:10–4:30 | Canada | Canada contributes six hundred forty-two point six terawatt-hours. Watch the bar approach the halfway line, then stop just short. Twenty-two countries together produce about four thousand nine hundred fifty-one. Half of China’s figure is slightly more than that. |

| 4:50–5:10 | Japan | Japan contributes one thousand fifteen terawatt-hours. | 5:10–5:30 | United States | Now the United States: four thousand three hundred ninety-one terawatt-hours. This addition pushes the team past three-quarters, then across China’s total. We finish at about eleven thousand one hundred eight terawatt-hours, around eleven percent above our target. |
| 5:30–5:50 | Final comparison | For this selected lineup, the twenty-fifth country takes us past China’s annual electricity generation. A different selection would produce a different answer. **Синхронизация с картинкой.** В каждом шаге:
- **0–1,7 с** — перелёт камеры;
- **2,2 с** — название страны;
- **2,7–3,8 с** — счётчик TWh;
- **6,6 с** — прибавка к шкале;
- **7,3 с** — комментарий на экране.

У США шкала растёт дольше (6,6–9,0 с): сначала проходит 75%, потом 100%. Голос удобно начинать с названия страны, примерно на 2 с от начала шага.
