## Description
How many countries would it take to match China’s electricity generation? We combine 25 selected countries — from Ireland to the United States — and watch a shared counter climb toward China’s 2024 total of about 10,025 terawatt-hours.

This is a selected lineup, not the minimum number of countries needed. After Japan, 24 countries reach 67% of China’s total. The United States takes the team to 110.8%.

One terawatt-hour (TWh) equals one billion kilowatt-hours. We compare annual electricity generation. This is not consumption, exports, power-plant capacity, or total energy supply. A larger generation total does not by itself mean a cleaner or more reliable power system.

Data: Ember, yearly electricity data, 2024, Total generation (TWh), country or economy level, snapshot downloaded 20 October 2026. Calculations use unrounded values; numbers on screen are rounded for display. Map boundaries are shown neutrally and do not imply any position on disputed territories.
